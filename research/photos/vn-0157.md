  another man's hand. The narrative (felling trees by the helicopter pad, the blasting cap, the

  is his held account and is not retold in an editorial field.
- **`location`** removed. `nui-ba-den` was where he says the injury happened ("Location: Nui Ba

- **`relatedPhotos`** emptied. VN-0041 was linked only through his held "maybe this is the day"

## His account, as it stood on the record (2026-09-05 and 2026-09-12)

Given at the photograph on 2026-09-05 (`giscus-vn-0157-c18308344.json`, transcribed), unasked,
with the location, the timeframe "Jan/Feb/Mar of 1965" and the description of the picture
itself, "The picture is him in the helicopter laying on the back floor." They were cutting trees
with explosive cord near the helicopter pad; he had a belt clip of caps and, in that telling, had
dropped one; the Cambodian picked it up and was bringing it to him and it blew some fingers off.
They kept his arms up to slow the bleeding, flew him to the Vietnamese army hospital by the base,
where ARVN nurses came out and took him; Larry did not think Americans could go in, flew away, and
never learned what became of him. He did not know the man well: "This is a different Cambodian
than the one always hanging out with me." His own closing question: "Do they have a Cambodian
area in there with Cambodian nurses?"

**Revised 2026-09-12, twice.** In his words on the stories page (`giscus-stories-c18415582.json`,
transcribed): "The story of the detonator, it may not have been one I dropped but the Cambodian
found one. I have no idea where he got it from." In his son's words on the photo page
(`giscus-vn-0157-c18415150.json`, paraphrase): "It's unknown if the detonator was dropped by my
father or was just found by the Cambodian." The 2026-09-05 account is not withdrawn ("may not
have been"), so both stand and whose cap it was is open in his own telling. The injury, the
flight and the hospital are unchanged between them.

## The sensitivity decision (#22)

The interview guide had held this frame "for the right moment" and said his answer would decide
whether it is shown publicly. He answered at the photograph on 2026-09-05, and the records then
said the decision was due and was the family's, with the 2026-09-12 revision as part of what it
should weigh. **Decided 2026-09-14 by the family:** keep the photograph public, hold the story.
The guide's gentle question to him ("Do you want it there for people to see…?") is unchanged and
is the guide's to retire; nothing here changes it. To show the story later: delete the `hold`
on the four recollections, then decide separately whether any of the above goes back on the
record's rendered fields.
