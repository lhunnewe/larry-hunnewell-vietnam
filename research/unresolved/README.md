# Unresolved questions

Working notes for the archive's open research questions. Each question gets its own file as
research begins. The public summary lives on the website's Research page; conclusions only move
into `data/` when the evidence supports a classification change.

Open questions:

1. **The KIA report** — locate the Stars and Stripes item (~August 1965, Pacific edition), any
   correction, casualty documentation, and the explanation for the error.
2. **The unit** — verify or correct "118th AOD — Aviation Operations Detachment"; explain the
   remembered 1st Cavalry association (note: 1st Cavalry Division arrived in-country ~Sep 1965).
3. **"Thom Son Nuht"** — identify the remembered Company Headquarters location. Hypothesis:
   Tân Sơn Nhứt. Consider other phonetic matches before settling.
4. **Sgt. Lowry / Lowery** — Special Forces sergeant on Núi Bà Đen, spelling and identity.
5. **Captain "Guegler"** — spelling, identity, and possible connection to the KIA report.
6. **Bombs jettisoned at sea** — separate documented procedure, ROE, aborted missions, political
   restriction, and rumor.
