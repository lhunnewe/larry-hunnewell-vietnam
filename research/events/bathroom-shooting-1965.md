# The bathroom shooting — when it happened (not settled)

**Research note, 2026-09-14.** Moved out of `data/timeline/1965-02-bathroom-shooting.json` during
the timeline cleanup that followed the site audit (`research/site-audit-2026-09-14.md`, "/timeline/").
Ledger #11, issue #59. Nothing new was searched for this note; it collects the dating evidence
already in the archive so the timeline record no longer has to carry it.

**Handling (from the interview guide, "Hold for the right moment"): put neither date to him, and
don't ask when the other unit arrived. He has said.** Nothing below goes in a field that renders.

## His words on the date, in the order he gave them

1. **2026-08-13, VN-0002** (`data/recollections/giscus-vn-0002-c18006961.json`, transcribed):

   > Guesses on dates for both shooting and picture daten, been there at least 6 months, guessing Feb or March

   One guess, covering the photograph and the shooting together, and hedged twice ("guesses",
   "guessing"). This is where the timeline's old heading "About February–March 1965" came from.
2. **2026-09-12, VN-0017** (`data/recollections/giscus-vn-0017-c18415788.json`, transcribed):

   > The other Unit is somewhere in the background, the guy that did the shooting in the bathroom.
   > They had just arrived maybe a week or so. The photo would probably be July of 1965.

   **His son, who typed it, clarified the same day:** "They had just arrived maybe a week or so" is
   when the other unit got there — about a week before the photograph — and is not a date for the
   shooting.

## How the two sit together

- Read with his son's clarification, the second account puts the shooter's unit on the base from
  about late June or early July 1965. If the shooter was with that unit when he fired, the shooting
  came after that — which does not fit "Feb or March".
- **One reading the archive had not written down:** "been there at least 6 months" is a lower
  bound, not a month. Counted from an arrival of about September–October 1964 it gives March–April
  1965 *or later*. The lower bound itself is compatible with July; it is the "guessing Feb or March"
  beside it that is not. This does not settle anything — both are his estimates — but it explains
  why the old heading looked firmer than his words.
- Both estimates are in 1965. That, and no more, is what the timeline now displays.
