# Research

Working research notes, organized by subject. This directory holds the messy middle of the
process — leads, transcriptions, correspondence notes, and drafts. Settled findings are reflected
in `data/` (structured records shown on the website) with citations in `data/sources/`.

- `people/` — research on individuals (Sgt. Lowry/Lowery, Captain "Guegler", fellow soldiers)
- `places/` — research on locations ("Thom Son Nuht", Núi Bà Đen activity, Nha Trang facilities)
- `units/` — order-of-battle research (118th AOD / 118th Aviation Company, AODs, 1st Cavalry)
- `events/` — event research (the KIA report, Rolling Thunder context, bomb-jettison stories)
- `unresolved/` — the open-questions ledger

Ground rules: never overwrite Larry's recollections; record provenance for every document; date
research notes; prefer primary and contemporary sources.
