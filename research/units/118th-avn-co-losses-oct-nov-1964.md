# The 118th Aviation Company's dead, October–November 1964

**Found:** 2026-09-09, incidentally, while enumerating Coffelt for the drowned-medic question
(#61). Bears on **#10 / ledger #2** — "verify the unit: 118th AOD and the 1st Cavalry
association."
**Source:** The Coffelt Database, December 2005 Update, via NARA AAD (`dt=1806`), Actual Date of
Death between 10/01/1964 and 11/30/1964 — 46 records, the complete set for those two months,
read in full.
**Provenance:** desk search by Claude Opus 5 in session with Larry's son. No new testimony.

## Why this matters

Larry arrived in Vietnam about September 1964. **Five men of the 118th Aviation Company died in
his first two months in country**, and the Coffelt record gives the unit chain in full — which
is the thing ledger #2 has been trying to pin down since August.

## What it confirms

**The chain `118th Aviation Company → 145th Aviation Battalion → 12th Aviation Group` is now
attested in a casualty database, not only in unit-history prose.** That is the same chain
`research/units/145th-avn-bn-tan-son-nhut.md` reconstructed, and it is independent
corroboration of it.

It also cross-checks the loss table already built for #89 from the VHPA panels, which recorded
*"7 Oct 1964 | UH-1B 63-08598 | 5 | Long Khánh, YS570950"* and *"1 Nov 1964 | — | 4"*. Coffelt
shows **three** 118th men on 7 October and **two** on 1 November. The counts differ because the
VHPA figures are per-aircraft and include men whose parent unit was not the 118th; both are
consistent, and neither is wrong.

## A caution about the "1st Avn Bde" column, which is a trap

Coffelt lists four of the five men under **1st Aviation Brigade** and one (Bain, same day as two
of the others) under **MACV**. **The 1st Aviation Brigade was not activated until March 1966** —
more than a year after every death in this table. Coffelt is retro-assigning the parent command
its records carry at compilation time, not the chain in force in 1964.

**Do not read this as evidence about what Larry's unit was called in 1964**, and do not let the
inconsistent Bain row be treated as a meaningful distinction between him and the men who died
beside him. The reliable parts of the chain here are **company, battalion and group**.

## What it does NOT do

It does **not** settle the "118th AOD" question. Larry remembers an **Aviation Operations
Detachment**; every record here says **Aviation Company**. That mismatch, and the Tân Sơn Nhứt
vs Biên Hòa location mismatch already in ledger #2, are untouched by this find.

Nor does it place Larry. Five men of a company dying in his first two months is context, not
evidence of his membership.

## What it opens

**Five named men, with home states, who were in the 118th while Larry says he was.** If he was
in that company, these are deaths that happened around him — and three of them on a single day
five weeks after he arrived. Two uses:

1. **A dating and membership probe, if it can be asked without priming.** Not "do you remember
   Bain, Havemann and La Flemme?" — that supplies the answer. Something closer to: *did anything
   happen to the unit soon after you got there?* **This note is deliberately not summarised into
   any rendered field** (rule 6).
2. **Next-of-kin and unit-association outreach** for #23, with five more names than the roster
   had.
