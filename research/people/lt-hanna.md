# Lieutenant Hanna — a pilot from Larry's unit, "killed in 1968 Kontum"

**Research note, 2026-09-05.** Testimony: `data/recollections/giscus-vn-0046-c18308860.json`
(transcribed, 2026-09-05, at VN-0046). Record: `data/people/lt-hanna.json`. Ledger #31, issue #126.

## What Larry gave

> Lieutenant Hanna, he was 19 or 20. He was kind of cross eyed. he would look at you and his
> eyes were cross eyed. he was a real good pilot. I flew with him a couple times. He was killed
> in 1968 Kontom which is a bout 50 miles south of Pleikeu. Hanna was part of my Unit. After I
> left, I guess he went onto another unit. I think he was 1st cavalary.

Five handles: **surname, year of death (1968), place (Kontum), division (1st Cavalry, hedged
"I think"), and that he was a pilot in Larry's own unit in 1964–65, aged about 19–20.**

Also in the same comment, separately: "Pleikeu was 1st Calvaries base. They called it the golf
course. I put the first radio beacon in there." The Golf Course was the 1st Cavalry Division's
base at **An Khê**, not Pleiku, occupied from September 1965 — after Larry's medevac. Either he is
remembering something he did at Pleiku for an earlier tenant, or the association is a later
overlay. Held against the unit question (#2), not pursued here.

**Neither fits.** The one that matches "1968, 1st Cav, pilot, killed" fails on the one thing that
cannot flex — he was a schoolboy when Larry was flying. Recorded as a scoped negative: the roll is
complete for helicopter pilots and crew, so **if Larry's Hanna was a helicopter pilot who died in
1968, he is not on it under this spelling.**

Note what Gary Hanna's entry *could* explain: a man who died in 1968 with the 1st Cav, widely
reported, whose surname Larry might later have attached to the young pilot he knew. That is
speculation and should not be put to Larry.

## What could still be true

- **Rank.** A 19–20-year-old pilot in 1964 would almost certainly have been a **warrant officer**,
  not a lieutenant; "Lieutenant" may be Larry's promotion of him. Search both.
- **Aircraft.** "Pilot" in Larry's unit could mean **fixed-wing** — his radio outpost was tied to
  aviation operations, and an O-1 or Caribou pilot would not be on the VHPA roll at all.
- **Spelling.** Hanna / Hannah / Hana / Hanner. The sound is the evidence.
- **Year and place.** 1968 and Kontum are each one memory; a man killed at Kontum in the Tết or
  post-Tết fighting is plausible, and so is 1967 or 1969.
