# The drowned medic from Nebraska (Nha Trang) — search record (2026-08-16)

**Larry's account (VN-0021 comment, 2026-08-15):** "A medic died here that was from
Nebraska, went out to the water and disappeared. Nha Trang beach hut #3 or #4."

**Status: comprehensive negative against the official casualty record.** No casualty
matches all three anchors (Nebraska + medic + drowned in Larry's tour window). Details
below so the search is not repeated; interview follow-ups added to the breakfast guide.

## What was searched

1. **NARA DCAS casualty list, home of record Nebraska** (archives.gov ne-alpha.pdf, all
   399 Nebraska fatal casualties): only 14 deaths dated 1964–65, none in 1964. The four
   inside Larry's in-country window (arrived ~Sep 1964, medevaced ~early Sep 1965):
   - LT William Marshall Roark, USN — 7 Apr 1965 — A-4 pilot shot down off Đồng Hới. No.
   - 1LT Nicolaus August Doeden, USMC — 6 Jun 1965 — helicopter crash. No.
   - LCpl Richard Lansing Zichek, USMC — 15 Jul 1965 — small-arms KIA. No.
   - SSG Harold William Shrader — 9 Aug 1965 — MACV advisor, grenade KIA. No.
2. **Full 58,220-record DCAS narrative file** (PalmBeachPost/Vietnam vnusreport.txt,
   derived from the NARA extract) — every entry containing "drown":
   - **No drowning with a Nebraska hometown before 1967.** The only Nebraska drowning
     near the era: PO3 **Herbert Allen Hilfiker** of Bloomfield NE, NMCB-10 Seabee,
     **6 Dec 1965** — after Larry left Vietnam, not a medic, and NMCB-10 was at Da Nang.
   - **No medic/corpsman drowned in-country during Larry's window at all.** Nearest:
     Sgt Leander Griffin (NY), medical specialist, MACV Advisors, drowned (KIA category)
     23 Oct 1965 — after Larry's departure, New York.
   - Drownings inside the window, any state/MOS: Sgt Tommie D. Emert (TX), Special
     Forces, 13 Dec 1964 — but his detachment was A1-112 at **Gia Vực** (Quảng Ngãi), not
     Nha Trang; SP4 Norman Elliott Jr. (VA) 20 Feb 1965; SP4 Moon Wai Lock (WA) 28 Jun
     1965; PO3 Daryl Keen (CA, Navy) 16 Jul 1965; Sgt Gilbert Smith Jr. (FL), 101st Abn,
     24 Aug 1965 — initially the only candidate with a plausible location (the 101st's
     1st Bde was in the Cam Ranh/Nha Trang area from Jul 1965). **RULED OUT 2026-08-16
     (browser session):** his Coffelt Database detail record (B Co, 2nd Bn, 327th Inf,
     101st Abn; casualty codes C3-J-7) gives the event as "DROWNED WHILE SWIMMING IN SONG
     BA RIVER 2 KM NORTH-NORTHWEST OF **AN KHE** 24AUG65" — Bình Định Province, not Nha
     Trang. **Every drowning in Larry's window is now located; none was at Nha Trang.**

## Possible explanations (all open)

- **"From Nebraska" ≠ home of record.** DCAS records HOR; a man born/raised in Nebraska
  could carry another state. If Larry confirms Army + fall '64, the next pass is
  drownings in the window regardless of state, checking birth state via findagrave.
- **"Medic" was Larry's gloss** for a Navy corpsman/Seabee or for someone whose actual
  MOS differed.
- **Date drift** — if it happened on a later beach visit (spring/summer '65), Smith
  (Aug 1965, 101st) is the closest single record.
- **Body-not-recovered cases** can carry late declared-dead dates; none matching was
  found, but a missing-presumed-dead search by province has not been possible yet
  (NARA AAD renders no record rows to non-browser clients; Coffelt province/cause fields
  need either a browser session on aad.archives.gov series dt=1806 or the record-detail
  pages).
