# Veterans History Project — first sweep, 2026-09-01

Opened by the accident recorded in `../places/vhp-chivers-a324.md`. Ledger #20, issue #70.

**Everything below is published archival description from the Library of Congress.** Names
and collection numbers are theirs, public, and citable. **No contact details are recorded
here and none should be**, per this archive's privacy rule on living people.

## Method, so this is repeatable

The LOC website rate-limits browsing and served 503s within a few page loads. **The JSON API
does not.** Every search below was run as:

```
https://www.loc.gov/collections/veterans-history-project-collection/?q=<term>&fo=json&c=100
```

with a browser User-Agent, then filtered locally. Each result carries a full
`item.service_history` block — dates of service, branch, rank, unit, location — so **service
dates can be filtered client-side without opening a single item page.** That is the trick
that made this sweep possible in one pass.

## The mountain, complete: only one man overlaps Larry's year

Of the **12 collections** that mention Núi Bà Đen or Black Virgin Mountain, service dates
break down as:

- **1962–69 — Terona Chivers**, Sergeant, **5th Special Forces Group** — the A-324
  photograph. The only Special Forces veteran of the mountain in the VHP whose service spans
  Larry's year.
- **1964–69 — Gary Stanford Hauge**, CW3, 74th Aviation Company; 205th Assault Support
  Helicopter Company. Dates overlap; the Vietnam portion is unspecified.
- **All ten others begin in 1966 or later** — 25th Infantry Division, 196th Light Infantry
  Brigade, 199th LIB, 1st Cavalry, Air Force reconnaissance.

That distribution is itself a finding, and it matches everything else learned today: **the
mountain only became a crowded American place after Larry left.** In 1964–65 it was a half
A-team and a radio relay; the veterans who wrote it into their oral histories were mostly
there for the war it became.

## What this does and does not do

**Does:** open a free archive of ~99,000 veteran collections, with a working search method,
and produce four collections worth reading that sit squarely in Larry's window and world.

**Does not:** name anyone Larry names. No Lowry, no Gagne, no Guegler, no Kennedy has been
searched for here yet — that is the obvious next pass and it is a different query shape
(search by veteran name rather than by place).
