# Hunnewell family in the newspapers — first sweep (2026-08-16)

Run on the family's newspapers.com trial after the family supplied the 1962–65 home
address (8815 12th Avenue, Inglewood, Calif.) and the parents' names: **Lester Arthur
Hunnewell** and **Geraldine Ruth Hunnewell, née Grosvenor**. Newspapers.com image IDs
cited so items can be re-opened directly. Living-relative details kept minimal.

## Geraldine in Inglewood — the Morningside Toastmistress Club

- *The Southwest Wave* (Los Angeles), **Jan 17, 1960**, p. 8 (image 732161881):
  "Morningside Toastmistress club slates installation" — officers installed at 3425 West
  Manchester Blvd.: "Mrs. Winifred Harmon, president; Mrs. Wallace Brown, vice-president;
  **Mrs. Lester Hunnewell, secretary**; and Miss Rita E. Ackerman, treasurer... the
  pledge of allegiance led by Mrs. Hunnewell."
- *The Southwest Wave*, **Jan 5, 1961**, p. 33 (image 732181264): Toastmistress meeting
  item — "**Mrs. Lester Hunnewell** will present the inspirational message."
- Unread, same paper: Apr 3, 1960 (p. 6) and a second Jan 5, 1961 match (p. 33) —
  "Lester Hunnewell" hits, likely the same club circle.
- Context: Morningside is the Inglewood neighborhood; the Wave was the LA
  southside/Inglewood community weekly. So while Larry was in high school and then in
  Vietnam, his mother was a club officer whose meetings made the society page.

  Note Birthday At Reunion**" — OLNEY — family reunion of the Hunnewells at the home of
  Mr. and Mrs. **Will Hunnewell**, celebrating the **78th birthdays** of Hunnewell and
  his **twin brother Walter** (both b. ~1884). Attending: Walter Hunnewell and sister
  Grace Fitzpatrick (Billings); sister Emma Brown; Mr. and Mrs. Theo Tillett (Rollins);
  Walter Ingraham family (Seeley Lake); Robert Gress family (Olney); son **James

- *The Daily Inter Lake*, **Jul 9, 1980**, p. 34 (image 1076386028): "The Crib Crowd"
  birth announcement, now transcribed in full (2026-08-17): "**Holly Marie Hunnewell** —
  Larry and Betty Hunnewell of **Bigfork and Missoula** are the parents of Holly Marie,
  born June 30 in Kalispell. Holly weighed 5 pounds 10 ounces and was 19 inches long.
  **She joins Larry, 5½, and Jennie, 3¾.** Holly's paternal grandparents are **Lester
  Hunnewell of Downey, Calif., and Geraldine Hunnewell of south[ern] California**"

  Behrens** and **Helen Behrens of Torrance, Calif.** Great-grandmother **Eloise
  Wickham** lives in Kalispell." One item names three generations on both sides: the
  sibling ages date Larry W. to ~Dec 1974/Jan 1975 and Jennie to ~Sep/Oct 1976; the
  family confirmed (2026-08-17) that Holly is the only Montana-born child — the others,
  including Leroy (b. Torrance, ~1982), were born in southern California. It also places
  Larry in **Flathead County in 1980**, explaining the Montana county recorder holding
  his certified DD-214.

## Larry's Inglewood youth — the motorcycle scrapes (found 2026-08-17)

Every "Larry Hunnewell" hit in California newspapers is Larry himself, 1951–1959:

- *South Gate Press*, **Sep 1, 1957**, p. 1 (image 744618031): "**Larry Hunnewel[l] of
  Inglewood** was shaken u[p] shortly after 4:15 p.m. when his **motorcycle collided
  with a car** driven by Utah Sutterfield, 48, of 9401 San Miguel Avenue. Hunnewell was
  traveling east on Firestone and the Sutterfield car was heading south on Sa[n
  Miguel]..." — Larry at ~16, on the front page.
- *The Daily Breeze* (Torrance), **Jul 10, 1959**, p. 3 (image 1094385223): "An
  **Inglewood motorcyclist** is being treated today in Daniel Freeman Hospital for a
  concussion and face and head cuts... **Thrown Out** — **Larry B. Hunnewell, 18**, was
  thrown to the pavement at **Crenshaw Blvd. and 120th St.** after his motorcycle
  collided with a vehicle driven by William T. Payne, 52, of Hawthorne... It first was
  feared the victim suffered a skull fracture and fractures of the right elbow, right
  leg, left collarbone and spine... condition today is 'good.'" *Caution: middle initial
  printed "B." (Larry's is D — Dennis); everything else fits — Inglewood, motorcycle, and
  the age: 18 in July 1959 matches Larry's documented 1940 birth exactly. Treat as
  strongly-supported, confirm at breakfast — "tell me about your motorcycle days in
  Inglewood" is now a Tier 3 interview question.*
- *The Southwest Wave*, **Apr 12, 1951** (images via search "Larry Hunnewell" us-ca;
  three matches, pp. 29/63): unread — Larry at ~10.

- Note: the user's sister **Jennie** shares her name with Hank's sister Jennie Hanson of

## The October 20, 1990 accident (recorded at the family's request, 2026-08-17)

Leroy asked that the coverage of the accident he and Holly survived be preserved.
*The Daily Inter Lake*, **Mon Oct 22, 1990, p. 1** — "Driver of hit-and-run car sought"
(image 1073853559, **clip 204017132**, full article): two fatal accidents near Kalispell
on Saturday night, Oct 20 — a hit-and-run on Montana 35 near McWenneger Slough that
killed Mary Treloar, 36, Bigfork, and, at 8:40 p.m. at **Helena Flats Road and East
Reserve Drive** (five miles north of Kalispell), a northbound pickup struck an eastbound
car broadside after the car missed a stop sign. "It was driven by **Mavis Treantos, 45,
Bigfork, whose daughter, Summer, 9, was killed. Injured were Holly Hunnewell, 10, and
Leroy Hunnewell, 8**, both passengers in the car. Bernard Wemple, 48, Kalispell, driver
of the pickup, was treated at the hospital and released... Holly Hunnewell [was] in
critical condition in the special care unit... while the Hunnewell boy was treated and
released."

**Summer Treantos' obituary** — *Daily Inter Lake*, Oct 22, 1990, p. 6 (image
1073853587, **clip 204018372**): "Summer Treantos, 9, Bigfork, died Saturday night of
injuries from an automobile accident in the Kalispell area. She has lived in the Bigfork
area except for a year in Denver, Colo. She is survived by her parents, **George and
Mavis Treantos of Bigfork; two sisters; and three grandparents, including Cynthia
Treantos of Columbia Falls**. Memorial services... Saturday at the Little Brown Church,
Bigfork. Private urn burial will be on family property." Same page: Bigfork Funeral Home
notice pairing the two services at the Little Brown Church.

Further coverage, cited but not clipped: Great Falls Tribune Oct 21 p. 37 and Oct 23
p. 4; The Missoulian Oct 22 p. 1 and Oct 23 p. 8; Helena Independent-Record Oct 22 p. 2
and Oct 24 p. 6; Daily Inter Lake follow-ups Oct 23 p. 5, Oct 24 p. 7, Oct 26 p. 5.
