**Subject: Re: reference request — Pictomap sheets 6231-4 N/S, and item 640VI4583**

Dear Vietnam Center & Archive,

Thank you for the very generous reply, and for the search strategies — I have already worked
through several of them and found more than I expected, including the September 1965 USOM
provincial reports and a 1965 issue of *The Observer*. The guides made the difference.

Two follow-ups, both from offers in your message.

**1. Pictomap sheets.** You mentioned that the 1:25,000 Pictomap L8020 supplement series is
not online but can be sent by email on request. The area I am researching is Núi Bà Đen and
Tây Ninh city, which falls on 1:50,000 sheet **6231-4**. Could you send the two Pictomap
halves — **6231-4 N and 6231-4 S**?

I am trying to identify the settlements immediately around the mountain in 1965, and an old
French fort my father describes as being "on the back side" of it. A 1:25,000 pictorial
sheet looked like the best chance of naming them.

**2. A film that is not digitized.** Item **640VI4583**, "DVD - UTT Helicopter Company
Vietnam 1964 - 1965," in the Jack Johnson Collection, is listed as NOT DIGITIZED. Its
description mentions footage of Núi Bà Đen, Tây Ninh, Vietnamese villages, and Special
Forces compounds and camps.

My father was a U.S. Army radio operator detached to the Special Forces camp on Núi Bà Đen
in 1965, and I have been reading the UTT/68th memoirs in your collection (items 0690505003
and 0690504006), which place that unit over the mountain in exactly those years. Film of
those places from that period would mean a great deal to him and to the record we are
building. Is a digitization or viewing copy possible, or is this something we would need to
come to Lubbock to watch?

**If there is any cost attached to either request, I would be grateful if you could tell me
the amount before doing the work** — I am glad to pay reasonable fees, I would just like to
know the figure first.

Anything you send will be cited to the archive, with the item number and your credit line,
in the research record we are keeping for the family.

Thank you again for the time you have already given this.
